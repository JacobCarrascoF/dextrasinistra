Three sections, one guitar

Every exercise has its code, a level from I to V, a tempo range and a tip on why it is there.

Dextra

The hand that picks

Alternate, hybrid, sweep and economy, strumming and rhythm, scales and arpeggios, enclosures and voicings. With a pick, with fingers (p i m a) and hybrid.

Sinistra

The hand that frets

Independence, legato, stretches and position shifts, bends and vibrato, chords and barre, muting. Also scales, arpeggios, enclosures and voicings.

Both

The two in sync

Fretboard maps, enclosures and voicings with pick and fingers at once. Plus Tabula, the 11 × 11 interval challenge.

Many exercises exist in all three sections under the same number: first the picking hand alone (D-1204), then the fretting hand alone (S-1204), and finally both together (A-1204). Same music, one hand at a time.

A method, not a list of exercises

Practicing a lot is not the same as practicing well. The app decides the order, the time and the tempo, so all you have to do is play.

01

Cycle-based plans

Every week a core exercise comes in and stays for three: two weeks raising the tempo and one of mastery at 90 % of your record, with a test on the last day. Pass it and it leaves the plan; if not, it stays one more week.

02

The 4 rules of a clean run

The whole exercise, start to finish. Complete, nothing left out. It sounds good. In time. Only clean runs count toward your record.

03

The tempo adjusts itself

Clean: 4 BPM more next time. Controlled: you keep it. Tense: you drop 10 %. No guessing what speed to work at.

04

Your real time, not the ideal

Every plan day as a full session or a short Snack. Whatever you don't finish is saved and you pick it up another day, on this device or another.

How it works

Five screens and nothing to set up. This is a normal day with the app.

01

Open Home and decide in one tap

Today's plan is at the top: choose the full session or a Snack depending on the time you have. Below, the warm-up, the weekly challenge and free practice with your last exercise.

02

Play with the tab in front of you

Tab, metronome and timer on one screen, with a bar that runs along the tab while you play. Hold − or + to change the tempo fast, tap Listen to hear the pattern and turn your phone to see it big. In voicings you see the chord and its frets.

03

Say how it went

When the time is up you mark Tense, Controlled or Clean. To count as Clean, all four rules. The app works out next time's tempo.

04

Follow the plan day by day

Every day has its session. When you finish the last exercise you choose to go on to the next day or stop for today, and you can run several plans at once.

05

See how you're doing

Minutes per day and per hand, streak, clean runs and each exercise's path to its goal tempo. And you can share your week.

Tabula: 121 interval pieces

Each piece combines an interval built on the root with the distance between roots, in 6 variants: 726 exercises for both hands. A piece is complete when its 6 variants are clean at 100 BPM or more.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up and no password: open the app and start playing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After opening it once with a connection, it works in airplane mode or on Wi-Fi that isn't responding.

Where is my practice saved?

On your device. Your practice is not sent to any server. To move it to another phone, tablet or computer: Settings → Export backup, and Import on the other one.

Can I use Plus on several devices?

Yes. Activate the same code on your phone, your tablet and your computer.

Which guitar and which level?

Electric or acoustic, with a pick, with fingers (p i m a) or hybrid. From level I, with the first string crossings, to level V, with enclosures, drop 2 and drop 3 voicings and Tabula.

How much time do I need a day?

The plans go from 25 to 60 minutes, 5 days a week. A Snack takes 10 or 20 minutes, and the custom plan starts at 10.

What happens when my Plus year ends?

Everything you logged stays. You go back to the free version until you renew.

Is it on the App Store or Google Play?

It's an installable web app: it installs from the browser in a few seconds and takes up no extra space.
