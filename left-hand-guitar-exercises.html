Left-hand guitar exercises

Your fretting hand needs just enough pressure, independent fingers and small movements. These exercises train it step by step, without forcing, with the metronome as your guide.

What the fretting hand works on

  • Finger independence: each finger moving without dragging the others, especially the ring and little fingers.
  • Legato: hammer-ons and pull-offs, so notes sound without picking.
  • Stretches and position shifts: opening the hand and moving along the neck without cutting the sound.
  • Bends and vibrato: hitting the exact pitch and moving it with control.
  • Chords and barre: just enough pressure for every string to ring.
  • Muting: silencing the strings you're not playing with your spare fingers.

The spider exercise (1-2-3-4)

The classic for finger independence: one finger per fret, index to little finger, on every string. Start at 60 BPM, in eighth notes, with a relaxed hand.

G|-------------------------1-2-3-4-|
D|-----------------1-2-3-4---------|
A|---------1-2-3-4-----------------|
E|-1-2-3-4-------------------------|

1 index · 2 middle · 3 ring · 4 little finger. Carry on the same way on the B and high E strings, then come back down.

Variations once it's clean: 1-3-2-4, 1-4-2-3, or start at the 5th fret and then move to the 1st, where the frets are wider.

Legato: hammer-ons and pull-offs

Pick only the first note of each group and sound the others with your fingers. All three notes should be equally loud.

G|-5h7p5-----5h7p5-----|
D|-------7h9p7-----7h9-|

h hammer-on · p pull-off.

Three rules to avoid injury

  1. Just enough pressure. Press only until the note sounds clean; more doesn't help.
  2. Fingers close to the strings. The less they lift, the faster and cleaner you play.
  3. If it hurts, stop. Tension in the hand or forearm is the signal to drop the tempo by 10%.

Frequently asked questions

How long until finger independence improves?

With 10 minutes a day, most people feel their ring and little fingers loosen up within three or four weeks.

Are chromatic exercises music?

Not really, but they're the gym for your hand. Mix them with scales and arpeggios, which train the same and also sound good.

My fingertips hurt at first. Is that normal?

Sore fingertips at first, yes. Pain in the wrist or forearm, no: stop, relax your hand and lower the tempo.

Does it work for left-handed players?

Yes. If you play with the guitar flipped, your fretting hand is the right one: the exercises are the same.
